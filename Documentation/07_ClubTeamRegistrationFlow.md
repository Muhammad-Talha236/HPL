# Club Team Registration Process

## 1. Overview

An approved club can create and manage its own teams.

Since the club has already been approved by the Super Admin, the Club Admin does **not** need separate Super Admin approval to create a team.

The Club Admin can create the team, add its players, and manage the team through the Club Portal.

Team registration and competition registration are separate processes.

---

## 2. Requirements

Before creating a team:

* Club must be approved and active.
* User must have Club Admin access.
* Club must have permission to create teams.

---

## 3. Team Registration Flow

```text
Approved Club
      ↓
Club Admin
      ↓
Club Portal
      ↓
Create Team
      ↓
Enter Team Information
      ↓
Add Team Players
      ↓
Create Team
      ↓
Team Portal Available
```

No Super Admin approval is required for normal team creation.

However, Super Admin can still monitor, suspend, edit, or remove a team if there is a violation or issue.

---

## 4. Team Information

When creating a team, the Club Admin provides:

* Team Name
* Team Logo
* Gender
* Region
* District/City
* Home Venue
* Team Description
* Contact Information

### Gender

The team must be identified as:

* Men's
* Women's

This allows the platform to keep men's and women's competitions and statistics properly separated.

---

## 5. Team Player Information

The Club Admin can add players while creating the team.

Each player can have:

* Full Name
* Profile Photo
* Date of Birth
* Gender
* Position
* Jersey Number
* Contact Information
* Nationality
* Player Registration/ID Number
* Previous Club/Team
* Player Status

### Player Status

Example statuses:

* Active
* Inactive
* Suspended
* Transferred

The Club Admin can add, update, or remove players from their own team's squad.

---

## 6. Player Registration

Adding a player to a team means the player becomes part of that team's squad.

Player registration should remain separate from competition participation if the league later requires competition-specific player registration.

Example:

```text
Club
 ↓
Team
 ↓
Add Player
 ↓
Player becomes part of Team Squad
 ↓
Team registers for Competition
 ↓
Players become eligible for that Competition
```

---

## 7. After Team Creation

Once the team is created:

* Team appears under the Club Portal.
* Team Portal becomes available.
* Club Admin can manage the team.
* Club Admin can manage team players.
* Team can view available competitions.
* Team can apply for competitions.
* Team can view fixtures, results, standings, and statistics.

---

## 8. Competition Registration

Creating a team does **not** automatically register it for a competition.

Competition registration is a separate process:

```text
Club Approved
      ↓
Create Team
      ↓
Add Players
      ↓
Team Created
      ↓
Select Competition
      ↓
Submit Competition Registration
      ↓
Super Admin Review
      ↓
Competition Approved
```

Super Admin approval is required at the **competition level** because the Super Admin needs to control which teams participate in a particular competition.

---

## 9. Club Admin Permissions

The Club Admin can:

* Create teams for their club.
* Update team information.
* Add/remove team players.
* Update player information.
* Manage team squad.
* View team fixtures and results.
* View team statistics.
* Register the team for available competitions.
* View competition registration status.

The Club Admin cannot:

* Manage another club's teams.
* Modify official league standings.
* Create official league matches.
* Approve competitions.
* Approve other clubs or teams.
* Change Super Admin settings.

---

## 10. Super Admin Permissions

Super Admin does not need to approve every team created by an already-approved club.

Super Admin can still:

* View all teams.
* Monitor team information.
* Suspend a team.
* Activate a suspended team.
* Remove a team when necessary.
* Manage competitions.
* Approve/reject competition registrations.
* Manage league-wide matches and standings.

---

## 11. Club-Owned Team Structure

A club can have multiple teams.

Example:

```text
Hunza United FC
│
├── Hunza United Men's Team
│   ├── Players
│   └── Competitions
│
├── Hunza United Women's Team
│   ├── Players
│   └── Competitions
│
└── Hunza United Youth Team
    ├── Players
    └── Competitions
```

The same club can therefore manage multiple teams while keeping each team's players, competitions, matches, and statistics separate.

---

## 12. Summary

```text
Club Registration
      ↓
Super Admin Approval
      ↓
Club Approved
      ↓
Club Admin Creates Team
      ↓
Add Team Players
      ↓
Team Created
      ↓
Team Portal
      ↓
Register for Competition
      ↓
Super Admin Competition Approval
      ↓
Team Participates in Competition
```

**Key Rule:**

> Super Admin approves the **Club** as a legitimate organization.
> Club Admin manages the Club's **Teams and Players**.
> Super Admin controls **competitions and league-wide operations**.
